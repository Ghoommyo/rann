# Rann — Privacy Policy (draft)

*Last updated: 2026-10-06. Review this before publishing, and host it at a public URL; both stores ask for one.*

**Short version:** Rann doesn't collect personal data, doesn't use accounts, ads or analytics, and doesn't sell anything about you.

## What the game stores on your device
- Your settings (volume, graphics, touch layout), in the app's private storage. They never leave your device.

## Online play
- When you host or join an online match, your device connects **directly** to the other player's device. To do that, the two devices exchange **IP addresses**, as every direct internet connection does.
- During a match, only game inputs (which buttons are pressed) and small sync checks are sent. Nothing is sent to us or stored on any server.
- If a match gets out of sync, a technical report is saved **on your device only** (no personal data).

## Children
The game doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
